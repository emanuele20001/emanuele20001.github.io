About

I was born in 2000 in Legnago, a small town in the province of Verona.

At nineteen, I moved to Milan, beginning my journey in the world of image and sound. I studied Design at the Politecnico di Milano, where I was taught the fundamentals of digital communication and the relationship between form and media content.

In 2022, after completing this first university cycle, I was admitted to the Civica Scuola di Cinema Luchino Visconti. Here, through the editing program, my predisposition for moving images found full expression and was nurtured both through theoretical knowledge of cinematic storytelling and technical mastery of dedicated software. In 2025, I earned my second degree, opening myself up to the world of cinematic storytelling.
